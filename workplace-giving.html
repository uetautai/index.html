Workplace giving

A regular gift from pay.

What this is

Workplace giving is a regular amount taken from salary and sent to BrokenStitch. It is usually set up by the employer’s payroll.

It is a gift to the published window. It is not a staff volunteering program unless that is agreed separately in writing.

How it is structured

  • Recurring — each pay cycle, set by payroll.
  • Pre-tax where the employer’s scheme allows it. BrokenStitch is DGR endorsed. ABN 15 693 395 970.
  • The staff member can usually stop it through payroll. We do not lock anyone in.

If the workplace cannot do payroll giving, a staff member can still use the monthly card option.

For the person

Ask payroll to add BrokenStitch. Give them the ABN. Keep your own record of the gift.

For the workplace

If you want BrokenStitch on your payroll-giving list, or you want a short note to circulate to staff, email us. Do not send us staff lists or young-person names.
