Workplace giving

After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth. Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. Logan, Queensland.

A regular pre-tax donation from pay, directed to after-hours youth response in Logan.

Workplace giving is a simple way to make a regular donation through payroll. A set amount each pay cycle is sent to BrokenStitch and supports After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth, Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm.

For workplaces

Workplace giving lets staff support one local organisation through payroll, without running a separate collection each month. Add BrokenStitch to your payroll-giving list and we will supply the charity details your payroll team needs.

If you need something staff can see internally, we can send a two-line note for an intranet: Staff gifts this year supported BrokenStitch’s after-hours response in Logan. Due to privacy and consent laws, we do not supply photographs, or display staff names on this websiite.

Payroll gifts are acknowledged by Gift received and the statement after 30 June, unless otherwise requested.

For individuals

Ask payroll to add BrokenStitch. The gift is tax deductible where you are eligible, and it can reduce your taxable income. If your employer reports the gift, you should not need a separate receipt at year end.
