Philanthropic giving

Substantial gifts, and gifts given over time, made in conversation with BrokenStitch.

Philanthropic gifts support After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth in Logan, Queensland — Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. People, families and foundations give once, or over several years, in the way that suits them.

If you would like to talk about a gift, email info@brokenstitch.org.au. Tell us whether you are writing as an individual, a family or a foundation, and whether you would like the gift acknowledged by name, by organisation, or not at all. Anonymous is the default. Names do not appear on this website unless you have asked, in writing.

The usual acknowledgement is a gift-received note, then a donor statement after 30 June. Eligible gifts are tax deductible. BrokenStitch is DGR endorsed. ABN 15 693 395 970.

For a gift in a will, we will confirm the legal name and ABN in writing. We do not publish that intention during a person’s lifetime unless they have asked.
