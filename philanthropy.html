Philanthropic giving

A gift that helps keep the after-hours window standing.

Philanthropy is how some people, families and foundations choose to give — a larger gift, or a gift that sits across more than one year. For some it is a calling. For others it is thanks. For some families it is the gift they want to leave behind them.

When you give this way to BrokenStitch, you are helping hold After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth in Logan: Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. You will know where the gift is going. You will have a person to speak with. Gifts are tax deductible. BrokenStitch is DGR endorsed.

If that is the kind of giving you have in mind, email info@brokenstitch.org.au and tell us you want to talk.

Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm · Logan, Queensland
