Philanthropic giving

A larger gift, with a conversation first.

What this is

Philanthropic giving is for people, families or foundations who want to give more than a card payment, or who want the gift shaped over time.

It is still a gift to the published window. It is not a project menu, a named place, or a promised result.

How it can be structured

  • One gift, after we have spoken.
  • A pledged amount over a set period.
  • A gift from a family, a private ancillary fund, or a foundation.
  • A gift in a will — we will confirm the legal name and ABN in writing.

The structure is agreed in writing before money moves, unless you simply use the card page.

What we will tell you

We will tell you the hours we can keep, and that the gift is tax deductible if you are eligible. We will not invent an outcome number or a story about a young person to close the gift.

Start here

Email info@brokenstitch.org.au with the subject “Philanthropic giving”.

Say whether you are asking for yourself, a family, or a foundation. Do not include a young person’s name.
