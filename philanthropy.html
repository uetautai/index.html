Philanthropic giving

After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth. Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. Logan, Queensland.

Philanthropic giving is for people, families and foundations who want to make a significant contribution to BrokenStitch. Some donors give once. Others give across several years. In each case we speak first, then confirm the gift in writing.

Funds support After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth in Logan, Queensland, Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. BrokenStitch is an ACNC-registered charity with Deductible Gift Recipient endorsement. Eligible gifts are tax deductible.

To begin, email info@brokenstitch.org.au with the subject line Philanthropic giving. Include your name, whether you are writing as an individual, a family or a foundation, and whether you would like the gift acknowledged by name, by organisation, or not at all. Anonymous is the default.

Thanks are private. We do not put names on this website unless you have asked us, in writing. Gift received, then a donor statement after 30 June, is the usual acknowledgement.

If you are writing about a gift in a will, we confirm the legal name BrokenStitch and ABN 15 693 395 970 in writing. We do not publish that intention while a person is alive unless they have asked.
