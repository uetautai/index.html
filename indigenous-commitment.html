Indigenous commitment

BrokenStitch acknowledges Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the First Peoples of this country. This page is a public commitment.

Acknowledgement of Country

BrokenStitch works in Logan, Queensland. We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the land, water and country now called the City of Logan, including Yugambeh, Yagara / Yugara, Yugarapul and Turrbal peoples. We pay our respects to Elders past and present, and to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples who live, work and belong here.

Overrepresentation

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children and young people are overrepresented in Australia’s child protection system, including in out-of-home care. Closing the Gap Target 12 is to reduce that overrepresentation. National reporting shows progress has not kept pace with that target.

Overrepresentation is a fact of the system. It is not a statement about Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander families. Safety includes culture, family, kin and Country.

Our commitment

  • The safety, protection and wellbeing of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander young people includes culture, family, kin and Country.
  • We listen to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander young people, families and Elders.
  • We take concerns seriously. We report as the law requires.
  • We work in the published window: Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. Logan, Queensland.
  • We do not claim a promised result.

Child safety reporting is on the Child safety commitment page.
