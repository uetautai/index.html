Child safety commitment

After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth. Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. Logan, Queensland.

BrokenStitch is committed to the safety, protection and wellbeing of young people. We listen. We take concerns seriously. We report as the law requires.

Child Safety & Wellbeing Policy, Child Safe Code of Conduct, Online Safety, and Complaints & Feedback are listed on About.

Report a concern to BrokenStitch

Choose the form that fits. You do not have to answer every question.

Young people If something has made you feel unsafe, or you want us to know something. Parents and guardians If you are worried about a young person, or you want us to follow up. Community members If you need to raise a concern or share information.
