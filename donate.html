WHAT YOUR MONEY DOES

Donations pay for Phase 1: case-by-case evening support, dual-worker contact, and the vehicle and operating costs required to hold the published window.

A donation does not buy a place, a reward, or a promised result.

BEFORE YOU DONATE

Does my donation actually go toward young people?

Yes. It funds the evening support they and their families can use: workers, transport and the safety systems around that contact.

How is the organisation accountable?

BrokenStitch is an ACNC-registered charity with DGR endorsement. Financial management and governance policies exist. Professional partners can request copies.

What if more money is raised than Phase 1 needs?

Surplus is held for the same purpose — keeping the published window reliable — or, later, for the next phase when funding capacity can hold it. We will not invent extra activity to spend it.

Do you have administration costs?

Yes. Insurance, two workers, records and a legal structure are part of delivering contact with young people safely.

Can I receive a receipt?

Yes. Gifts of $2 or more are generally tax deductible in Australia because of our DGR endorsement.

How is my information handled?

Payment data is handled by a third party company. Contact details used for a receipt are kept for that purpose and legal record-keeping. See privacy.

Who oversees the organisation?

BrokenStitch operates as a registered charity with written governance and conflict-of-interest policies. Contact info@brokenstitch.org.au.

How will I see what my donation has achieved?

We will not send impact statistics we cannot stand behind. What we can report is whether the published window was held, and at what scale.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Generally yes, for gifts of $2 or more, because BrokenStitch has DGR endorsement from the ATO.

What if a gift cannot be used immediately?

It is held and applied to Phase 1 costs, or retained until a later phase can be held reliably.
