Larger gifts

Some people want to give more than a card payment, or to give over time with a conversation attached.

That is welcome. It does not buy a place in the work, a say over who we take, or a promised result.

Email info@brokenstitch.org.au and say you want to talk about a larger gift. We will reply from that mailbox.

Organisations

Businesses and community groups can give money, give through the workplace, give in-kind for the published window, or ask for a written partnership.

A partnership is in place only when it is confirmed in writing. It does not purchase a place or a promised result. Referrals stay on the Referral page.

Anyone working with young people through BrokenStitch follows the Child safety commitment.

We review each enquiry. A partnership is in place only when it is confirmed in writing.
