WHAT WE DO

Phase 1 is practical evening support, case by case, Monday to Friday, 5:00 pm to 10:00 pm.

  • Support for a small number of referred young people and their families
  • Two workers present for face-to-face contact
  • Professionals contact us to talk through a referral

This is not a crisis line, drop-in, or walk-up service.

WHO WE HELP

Young people aged 13–18 on bail or Youth Justice orders in Queensland, starting in Logan. We work with their families.

Referral only — Staying On Track, Youth Justice officers and other known services. referrals@brokenstitch.org.au

WHY IT MATTERS

Most services close in the evening. This cohort still needs practical support at those hours.

We do not publish outcome statistics. We measure whether the published window is actually held.
