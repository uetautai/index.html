WHAT WE ARE

A registered charity providing after-hours crisis support and a safe house for young people on youth justice orders.

THE MEANING BEHIND THE NAME

A stitch is meant to hold things together. When a stitch breaks, the fabric starts to pull apart.

Broken Stitch represents the moment when support failed — not the person. Many young people who reoffend aren’t broken. The systems, opportunities, trauma responses and guidance that were meant to hold them together were. The broken stitch symbolises the gaps in education, mentorship, stability and belonging that caused lives to unravel.

But a broken stitch can be repaired — often stronger than before. In tailoring, when a stitch breaks you don’t discard the garment. You restitch it, reinforcing it so it won’t fail the same way again.

Broken Stitch exists to re-stitch lives. We provide structure, skills, accountability and belief where there were once gaps. The logo’s broken stitch pattern reflects both disruption and intentional repair — a visible reminder that growth comes from fixing what was overlooked, not throwing it away.

Our Approach

We are not the solution to the problems these young people face. Those problems run deeper than any single program can claim to fix.

As Thomas Sowell put it: there are no solutions — only trade-offs.

Broken Stitch offers practical trade-offs. We provide real alternatives that help meet the criminogenic needs that drive offending behaviour — structure, consistent presence, safe places to go, and people who will not walk away when the formal systems close for the night. We step into the gaps. That is the work.

WHAT WE DO

Practical support when it matters most

🏠

SAFE HOUSE

A place to go when there is nowhere else. Short-term respite accommodation that gives young people a safe, stable option instead of the street or further risk.

🕐

AFTER-HOURS SUPPORT

Support does not stop at 5pm. We provide outreach and crisis response when most services are closed — the hours when young people are most exposed.

🤝

CRISIS INTERVENTION

Immediate presence when things escalate. We step in quickly to de-escalate, stabilise and offer a different path in the moment.

👨‍👩‍👧

FAMILY ENGAGEMENT

Where it is safe and appropriate, we work with families and kin. Strong connections are part of the fabric that holds young people together.

🔗

REFERRAL PATHWAYS

We connect young people to the right supports — education, housing, health, and other services — so the re-stitching continues beyond our direct involvement.

🛡️

CULTURAL SAFETY

Particularly for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander young people, we prioritise cultural connection, respect and safety in everything we do.

📋

CASE SUPPORT

Consistent, dedicated support that builds structure and accountability over time — not one-off interventions that disappear when the crisis passes.

💬

DEBRIEFS & FOLLOW-UP

After a crisis we stay present. We check in, review what happened, and keep the relationship going so the young person is not left alone again.

🌱

ALTERNATIVES TO OFFENDING

We offer practical trade-offs that meet criminogenic needs differently — giving young people real options instead of the same pathways that lead back to reoffending.
