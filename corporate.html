Corporate partnership

After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth. Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. Logan, Queensland.

Partnerships are critical to our work. We cannot hold an after-hours window on our own.

Young people in Logan meet pressure from more than one direction. Answering that takes more than one organisation. When a business or community group walks with us, Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm has a better chance of standing.

Partners support After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth through a gift of money, workplace giving for staff, or practical support that keeps those hours possible.
