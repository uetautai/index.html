Corporate partnership

Organisations that want to stand behind the published window.

What this is

A corporate partnership is an organisation relationship. It is not a purchase of a place, a logo on a young person, or a promised result.

A partnership is in place only when it is confirmed in writing.

How it can be structured

  • A gift of money — one-off, monthly or yearly, including through Donate by card.
  • Workplace giving for staff — see Workplace giving.
  • In-kind support that helps hold Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm.
  • A written agreement for an ongoing relationship.

Referrals stay on the Referral page. Anyone working with young people through BrokenStitch follows the Child safety commitment.
