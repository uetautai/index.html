WHO WE ARE

BrokenStitch is an ACNC-registered charity in Logan, Queensland. Phase 1 is practical evening support for young people on bail or Youth Justice orders, and for their families.

  • Phase 1 starts with three to four young people
  • Support is case-by-case and developed with families
