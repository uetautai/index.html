Become a partner

BrokenStitch is an after-hours youth response for high risk youth in Logan, Queensland. Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm. Registered charity. DGR endorsed. ABN 15 693 395 970.

What a partner is

Organisations, businesses, and community groups that want to support the published window. A partnership does not purchase a place, a reward, or a promised result. Referrals are made on the Referral page.

What partnership can look like

  • A gift of money (DGR endorsed). Donate.
  • Regular giving, including workplace giving.
  • In-kind support for the published window.
  • Helping people in your organisation find the hours and the Referral page.
  • A written agreement for an ongoing relationship.

How to enquire

  • Email info@brokenstitch.org.au, or use the form below.
  • Include your organisation, a contact person, and what you would like to offer.
  • We review each enquiry. A partnership is in place only when it is confirmed in writing.
  • Anyone working with young people through BrokenStitch follows the Child safety commitment.
  • Gifts of $2 or more to a deductible gift recipient are generally tax deductible in Australia. A payment receipt is not a DGR receipt. DGR receipts are issued from info@ after a gift is received.
