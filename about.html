About

BrokenStitch. After-hours Youth Response for High Risk Youth. Monday–Friday, 5:00 pm–10:00 pm.

The place

BrokenStitch works in Logan. This is not a place we simply operate in. It is home to many families, communities and young people, and it has a history that began long before us.

We name this place: Logan, Queensland, along the river and the ridges.

Who stands here

We name the peoples whose old people belong here, where several Countries meet: Yugambeh, Yuggera (Yagara), Yugarapul, and Turrbal.

We pay our respects to Elders.

We name respect that are raised here, and the knowledge, culture and connection they continue to carry.

The old people

The old people come first. They hold the names, the manners, and the memory of this Country.

We honour those who have gone, and those who sit with us now.

Elders matter because young people matter. The people who came before us matter because the people who come after us matter.

How the young regard them

A living culture teaches a child where they stand by how they regard the elderly. You greet them. You listen before you speak. You look for their faces in the room. You carry their names with care.

A child who knows how to honour the old people knows they come from somewhere, and that they are held. That is belonging

We believe a young person should know where they come from, know who stands behind them, and know that their future is bigger than the circumstances they are facing today.

What we will not do

BrokenStitch comes here to support young people — not to speak for communities, claim their culture, or pretend we have all the answers.

How we walk here

We listen. We learn. We show up. Where a young person needs someone to stand beside them, we want to be there.

BrokenStitch comes as a guest. We do not claim this Country. We do not speak for them. We listen to families.
